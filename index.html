Bam Pokes Ants

Instructions

Poke the fire ant mounds with your stick, then run like crazy before the swarm gets you.

Arrows / WASD to move. Shift to run. Space pokes the nearest mound in reach. Pick up a flamethrower when one appears and it fires automatically until it runs out. On touch screens, drag to move, tap a mound to walk over and poke it, and double-tap to sprint for 4 seconds.
